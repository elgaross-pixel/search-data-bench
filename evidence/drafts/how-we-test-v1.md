# How Search Data Bench Tests Search, SEO and AI Data APIs

Our rule is simple: documentation can prove that a vendor describes a capability; it cannot prove how that capability performed in our test.

## Evidence hierarchy

**VERIFIED HANDS-ON** means we executed or directly observed the stated behavior and retained evidence.

**OFFICIAL DOCUMENTED** means the vendor's current primary documentation supports the statement, but we did not execute that behavior in the stated test.

**THIRD-PARTY** identifies outside evidence.

**NOT TESTED** means the capability may exist, but our stated test did not execute it.

**UNAVAILABLE / LIMIT** means we attempted the frozen task and a plan, quota, response or other observed constraint prevented the result.

**UNKNOWN** means the evidence is insufficient.

## Frozen inputs

Comparisons are designed before results are seen. We freeze query sets, market, language, device and evaluation rules. We do not replace inconvenient keywords or missing results after execution.

## No fake metric equivalence

KD, authority, trust and similar proprietary metrics can use different datasets and formulas. We retain provider labels and compare observable disagreement; we do not average them into a universal score.

## Missing data is evidence

A missing result is not zero. A plan limit is not a product-wide absence. A delayed result is not a failed feature. Those states remain explicit.

## Commercial independence

Affiliate links may fund the site. They do not change evidence grades, fill missing values or determine a benchmark result.
